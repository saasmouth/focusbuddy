PlexiDesk canvas

Every task gets its own desk.

A desk holds the notes, tables, browser windows, files, timers, sketches, diagrams and mind maps that belong to one piece of work, arranged wherever you want them. Open a desk and you drop straight back into that work.

  • Widgets for everything: sticky and Markdown notes, tables with six views, an embedded live browser, files, focus timers, a calendar, forms.
  • Think visually: mind maps with suggested branches, a pressure-sensitive scratchpad, and node-and-edge diagrams.
  • Arrange freely: link widgets with ghost lines, drill into one full-screen, pan in any direction, or auto-arrange in a click.
  • Build a desk from a sentence: describe what you need and Plexii lays it out.

Close a desk and the clutter goes with it. Open it tomorrow and you pick up exactly where you stopped.

PlexiOffice

A full office suite, on the canvas.

Docs, Sheets, Slides, Draw and Design each have the File, Edit, Insert and Format menu bar you already know, and every menu item does something real. Open your Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Visio files and hand them back the way you saved them.

  • PlexiDocs: real pages, Track Changes, comments with @mentions, export to Word, PDF or web.
  • PlexiSheets: a formula engine with LET and LAMBDA, charts, pivot tables, conditional formatting and sparklines. Describe a calculation and Plexii writes the formula.
  • PlexiSlides: charts driven by live sheet data, speaker notes, transitions and a present mode.
  • PlexiDraw and PlexiDesign: diagrams, brand-aware templates, a font library, stock photos and background removal.
  • A built-in browser for looking things up, and sending a page to a desk when it matters.

The sheet you're checking sits right beside the doc you're writing. No copy and paste, no hunting for the latest version.

Plexii assistant

An assistant that does the work and shows you how.

Plexii lives at the top of every editor and in its own hub. Ask in plain language and it answers from your documents, sheets, notes, tasks and tables across every desk, streaming the reply while a live trace shows what it read.

  • Citations you can open for every claim, and a plain "I couldn't find that" when it can't.
  • Review-and-apply cards: a table, a task, a calendar block or a whole document, each previewed before it lands.
  • Scope the question to the whole workspace or just the document you have open.
  • Multi-step work behind a consent gate, with a live run you can watch and a Stop button. It never signs in, pays or moves files for you.
  • Your key or ours: bring your own Anthropic key, or use managed Plexii on Pro with no setup. Usage and estimated spend are shown in Settings.

Live web search, assistant memory and the Plexii agent are included in Pro and Team.

Questions.

Is the free plan really free forever?

Yes, for personal use. You get unlimited desks, the full office suite, the Plexii assistant on your own Anthropic key, meetings, mail and chat, and sync across up to three devices.

Do I need an Anthropic API key?

On Free, Plexii runs on your own Anthropic key, so prompts go straight from your machine to Anthropic and you pay them directly. On Pro and Team, managed Plexii works with no key to set up.

Where is my work stored?

On your machine first. Cloud sync is opt-in and encrypted in transit, though not end-to-end encrypted. Data for features you choose to use, such as sharing, live collaboration, chat and mail, is stored on our servers so those features can work.

Which computers does it run on?

Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel on macOS 11 or later, using one universal installer. Windows 10 or 11 PCs with Intel or AMD processors. Windows 11 on Arm PCs run the standard Windows installer through built-in emulation; a native Arm build is on the way. Linux is coming too.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

See the refund policy for the details.

We're a large organisation. Can we self-host?

Not today. Self-hosting, private cloud, configurable data residency and enterprise audit logs are not shipped capabilities. Book a demo to review the deployment, security and migration requirements PlexiDesk supports now, and the gaps we would need to scope.

Installing on a Mac

  1. Open the .dmg. It shows PlexiDesk.app next to your Applications folder.
  2. Drag it to Applications, then eject the disk image.
  3. Launch it. The build is signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens without a warning.
  4. Grant Accessibility if you want SpeedDeck to send keystrokes to other apps. You can skip this.
  5. Sign in, or don't. Accounts add sync and sharing; the app works fully without one.

Installing on Windows

  1. Run the .exe.
  2. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Launch PlexiDesk and start your first desk.

One desk per task: why we built PlexiDesk

The Plexii team, October 2026, 4 minute read

Most of us don't do one kind of work. We do several pieces of work, each of which needs a document, some numbers, a few web pages, a conversation and a list of what's next. The software we use is organised the other way round: one app for documents, one for numbers, one for mail, one for chat, and a browser holding everything else in tabs.

So every piece of work gets spread across a dozen windows. Tabs get closed. Context is lost. Effort is duplicated. You rebuild the same picture of the work every time you come back to it.

Organise around the work, not the app

PlexiDesk flips that around. Every task gets a desk: a canvas where the doc, the sheet, the research, the notes and the people for that one piece of work sit side by side. The office suite, the browser, meetings and mail come to the desk, rather than the desk being scattered across them.

New task? Start a desk. Repeated task? Come back to the desk you already made. Collaborative task? Invite people onto it.

Closing a desk is the point

When the work stops for the day, close the desk. The clutter goes with it. Tomorrow, open it and everything is where you left it, with a one-line summary of where you stopped and what's next. No thirty-tab session you never look at again.

Why the assistant lives on the desk

Because the desk holds the work, Plexii, the assistant, can answer from it. Ask where the budget stands and it reads the sheet that's actually on the desk, cites it, and offers the next step as a card you approve. More on that in how Plexii shows its sources.

Show your sources, ask before you act: how Plexii is designed

The Plexii team, October 2026, 5 minute read

An AI assistant is only useful at work if you can trust what it says and stay in control of what it does. Plexii is built around two rules that follow from that.

Rule one: show the sources

Plexii answers from your own documents, sheets, notes, tasks and tables. Every claim carries a citation you can click through to the item it came from, and a live trace shows what it read while it works. When it can't find something, it says so rather than inventing an answer.

That changes how you use it. You don't have to take the answer on faith; you check the two or three sources it points to, which is far quicker than doing the research yourself.

Rule two: ask before acting

When an answer implies a next step, Plexii drafts it as a card: a task, a table, a calendar block, an updated slide, a whole document. Each card shows a preview of the real change. Nothing lands until you apply it, one card at a time or all at once.

The busywork disappears. The decisions stay yours.

For bigger jobs on Pro and Team, Plexii can do multi-step work for you: browsing pages, gathering data into a table, drafting a set of changes. That work runs behind a consent gate, with a live run you can watch, a running cost and a Stop button that always works. Before it touches a new site it asks whether to allow it once, always, or not at all. It never signs in, pays or moves files on your behalf.

Your key or ours

You choose how the assistant runs. With your own Anthropic key, prompts go straight from your machine to Anthropic with no proxy. With managed Plexii on Pro, requests run through our server, which records token usage for billing and never uses your prompts for training. Either way, usage and estimated spend are visible in Settings.

Local-first, explained: where your work lives in PlexiDesk

The Plexii team, October 2026, 4 minute read

"Local-first" gets used loosely, so here is exactly what it means in PlexiDesk, including the parts that do touch a server.

What stays on your machine

Your folders, desks, desk objects, notes and SpeedDeck set-up are stored in a local database on your computer. The app works fully offline and without an account. The vault is sealed with a master password only you know; we can't see it and we can't recover it.

What uses a server, and why

Some features only work if data is stored on a server: sharing a link, the cloud inbox that receives shares while your app is closed, sync across devices, live collaboration, chat, meetings and mail. When you use one of those, the data it needs is stored on our servers so it can work. Sync is encrypted in transit but not end-to-end encrypted, so the server can read what it stores for those features. You can revoke a share link at any time.

AI, two ways

With your own Anthropic key, prompts and responses go straight from your machine to Anthropic and never pass through our servers. With managed Plexii, requests run through our server, which records token counts and estimated cost for billing, and doesn't sell your prompts or use them to train models.

What we don't collect

No third-party analytics: no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment or ad trackers. First-party usage telemetry (app version, platform and aggregate counts, never your content or prompts) is on by default and can be turned off in Settings.
