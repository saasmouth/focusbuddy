The CSV is the deliverable. docs/plexii-feature-inventory.csv holds all 428 rows with the columns below, ready to load. This document explains what is in it and where each value came from — a seed nobody can audit gets distrusted the first time a row looks wrong.

Everything factual is read out of source by scripts/extract-feature-inventory.mjs: the widget catalogue, the View union, the segment registry, the IPC registrations, the store and engine layers. Re-run it and the inventory matches the code again.

The written parts are marked. Positioning is judgement and cannot be derived, so it is confined to the feature areas later in this document.

There are no importance weights in here, deliberately. Assigning 1-10 by feel would put invented numbers into a database meant to inform real decisions, and they would be indistinguishable from measured ones a month later.

What you get instead are three measured signals you can weight yourself: dependents (how many files import it — the blast radius of a change), surface (how many operations a capability exposes), and tests (how many test files name it). The weight column is empty and yours.

Cross-functionality

Links are not a separate table because they are already implicit in two columns and better recomputed than stored: dependents is the import graph, and a capability's namespace prefix maps to the stores and engines that call it. If you want an explicit edge list, the extractor already walks every file and can emit one — that is a small change to the script, not a new exercise.

Feature areas 12

The authored layer: positioning and change-impact notes for the areas worth selling and worth being careful with. Written, not derived.

The desk

Canvas · 55 widget kinds · layouts · wires

A canvas per piece of work, holding whatever that work needs — tasks, a table, a document, a live web page, an AI agent — side by side instead of scattered across apps.

Depends on: nodes, widgets, tables, documents
Impact of change: The most coupled surface in the product. A change to widget storage or layout reaches every desk and every widget kind; treat the widget contract as public API.

PlexiOffice

13 apps · 6 document types · Mail · Chat · Meet · Sign · Browser

Documents, spreadsheets, slides, diagrams, design and drawing in one place, plus the communication around them — and a web browser that does not need a desk.

Depends on: documents, mail, office shell, view store
Impact of change: Document types share one editor stack; a change to the editor reaches the standalone route, the desk widget and the Office shell at once.

Browsing agent

browserAgent · browserActions · browserOverlays · browserProgress · consent

Plexii browses for you — researching, comparing and extracting — visibly, one step at a time, stoppable, and with the results landing on a desk as a table you can use.

Depends on: Anthropic API, webview, consent store, tables
Impact of change: Per-site consent and the refusal rules are safety boundaries. Changing the action vocabulary changes what the model can do to a live page; the sanitiser is the gate.

AI assistant

anthropic · chat · action proposals · action executor

One assistant across the whole workspace that proposes changes you approve, rather than a chat box that writes text you then copy somewhere.

Depends on: Anthropic API, every store it can act on
Impact of change: The proposal vocabulary is the widest contract in the app: adding a verb adds something the AI can do everywhere, and every applier must handle it.

Mail

imap · mailPaging · mailTags · triage

Your inbox, filed against the work it belongs to — folders that relate to a desk or task, so a busy mailbox stops being one undifferentiated list.

Depends on: IMAP, nodes, AI triage
Impact of change: Paging is by UID rather than position; anything that assumes index ordering will break on a mailbox that changes under it.

Tables and data

tables · schema · rows · views (Table/Cards/Kanban/Calendar/Gantt)

A database that behaves like a spreadsheet, on a desk, fed by hand, by automations, or by what Plexii finds while browsing.

Depends on: tables store, widgets
Impact of change: Column types coerce values on write. Changing a type affects existing rows, so migrations matter more here than anywhere else.

Custom widgets

customWidget · sandbox · wizard · action vocabulary · helpers

Describe the tool you need and Plexii builds it, running sandboxed on your desk with access only to what you wire into it.

Depends on: Anthropic API, sandbox scheme, widget inputs
Impact of change: ADR-0009 is the boundary: unlimited in what a widget can BE, bounded in what it can REACH. Loosening the sandbox is a security change, not a feature change.

The tray and navigation

view store · openTray · segmentApps

Everything you have open in one strip — desks, documents, chats, apps — so moving between contexts is a click rather than a retrace.

Depends on: view store
Impact of change: The view union is the app’s routing contract. A surface that holds "what am I looking at" in local state disappears from the tray, from Back, and from session restore.

Automation

flows · agents · wires · signals · streamdeck

Standing workers on your desks: rules that run, agents that watch, and physical buttons that fire them.

Depends on: flows, agents, widget links
Impact of change: Saved flows persist verb names. Renaming a verb silently changes what every stored flow does — SPEC-044 exists because of this.

Knowledge and search

knowledge · brain map · decisions · search

What the workspace knows, linked: entries, a graph of how they connect, the decisions taken and what a change puts at risk.

Depends on: knowledge store, search index
Impact of change: Decisions reference work items; deleting the referenced item must not orphan the decision.

People and organisation

contacts · directory · org admin · people map · presence

Who is in the workspace, how they relate, and who is around right now.

Depends on: contacts, org, presence
Impact of change: Org structure feeds permissions; a change to roles reaches sharing and the vault.

Sharing and collaboration

shares · liveDesk · crdt · collaborations

Hand someone a desk — including someone who is not a user yet — and work on it together, live.

Depends on: crdt, shares, auth
Impact of change: CRDT merge rules decide who wins a conflict. Changing them changes history, not just behaviour.
